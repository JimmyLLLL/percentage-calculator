    Percentage Formulas

    • What is X% of Y? — Multiply Y by X/100. Example: 20% of 150 = 150 × 0.20 = 30.
    • X is what % of Y? — Divide X by Y, multiply by 100. Example: 30 is what % of 150? (30/150) × 100 = 20%.
    • Percentage change — ((new − old) / old) × 100. Positive = increase, negative = decrease.
    • Percentage increase — ((new − old) / old) × 100. Example: 80→100 = ((100−80)/80) × 100 = 25%.
    • Percentage decrease — ((old − new) / old) × 100. Example: 100→80 = ((100−80)/100) × 100 = 20%.

    How to Calculate Percentage Quickly

    To calculate a percentage mentally, a useful trick is to find 10% first (move the decimal point one place left), then multiply. For example, to find 15% of $40: 10% is $4, half of that (5%) is $2, so 15% = $4 + $2 = $6. Our calculator does this instantly for any numbers — no mental math required.

    FAQ

    What is a percentage? — A percentage is a number expressed as a fraction of 100. The symbol "%" means "per hundred." So 50% = 50/100 = 0.5.

    How is percentage different from a fraction? — A percentage is just a fraction with denominator 100. 25% = 25/100 = 1/4.

    Can percentages be over 100%? — Yes. 200% means twice the original amount. This is common in growth calculations.
